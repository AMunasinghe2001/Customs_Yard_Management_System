SRI LANKA CUSTOMS
SR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

TERMS OF USE

Read these terms. You must accept them to install this software.


1. WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS

This is the Seizure Report (SR) Management System of the Sri Lanka Customs
Department. It records seized, detained, abandoned and forfeited goods, their
storage, their valuation, their sale or destruction, and the officers who
acted on each of those.

It is an internal departmental system. It is not licensed, sold or offered to
the public.


2. WHO MAY USE IT

Only officers of the Sri Lanka Customs Department who have been issued an
account by the ICT Directorate.

Your account is yours alone. You may not share your password, sign in on
another officer's behalf, or let another person use a session opened with your
credentials. Every action this system records is recorded against the account
that performed it, and that record is used.


3. WHAT YOU MAY NOT DO

You may not:

  * install or run this software on a computer that is not the Department's,
    or outside a Customs yard or office, without written authorisation;
  * copy, distribute, publish or pass on this software or any part of it;
  * decompile, disassemble or otherwise attempt to derive its source code;
  * alter it, or connect it to any database other than the Department's;
  * extract, copy or disclose any data it holds, except as your duties
    require and your authorisation permits.


4. THE DATA IN THIS SYSTEM

Consignee names, goods, valuations, case numbers and officer records are
official Customs information. Some of it is commercially sensitive and some
of it identifies private individuals and businesses.

Treat everything you see here as confidential. Do not photograph, screenshot,
copy to personal storage, or send outside the Department any part of it
without authorisation. Your obligation continues after you leave the post or
the Department.


5. THIS SYSTEM KEEPS A RECORD

Sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts, account changes, and every change to a
record are written to an audit trail that cannot be edited or deleted.

That trail is kept for a year and may be examined in any inquiry.


6. SECURITY

  * Your password expires every 90 days and must then be changed.
  * You may not reuse a password you have used before.
  * The system locks itself after 5 minutes without activity.
  * An account that is not signed into for 30 days is disabled, and only the
    ICT Directorate can restore it.

Report a lost, shared or suspected password to the ICT Directorate at once.


7. NO WARRANTY

This software is provided as it is. The Department does not warrant that it
will be free of faults or available without interruption.

It supports the SR register; it does not replace the register, the Customs
Ordinance, or any officer's own duty to verify what they record. The
correctness of an entry remains the responsibility of the officer who makes
it.


8. BREACH

Using this software or its data outside these terms may be a disciplinary
matter, and may be an offence under the Customs Ordinance, the Computer Crimes
Act No. 24 of 2007, or other written law.
